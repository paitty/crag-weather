How the dryness score works
  • Runs an hour-by-hour water balance on the wall over the last 5 days and the next 5.
  • Wetter: rain (only ~20% reaches steep/sheltered walls), and condensation when the air sits at the dew point with no sun.
  • Drier: dry air (vapour-pressure deficit), wind, and sunshine on the wall's aspect at that time of day (east walls dry in the morning, west in the afternoon, north barely).
  • Rock type changes the speed (granite/gneiss faster, conglomerate/sandstone slower). Crags with ⚙️ have no known wall direction and use an average sun exposure. Rock is limestone unless set otherwise.
  • ✅ 75+ dry · 🟡 40-74 damp · 💧 under 40 wet. It's a model - seepage, cracks and local showers can still surprise you.
